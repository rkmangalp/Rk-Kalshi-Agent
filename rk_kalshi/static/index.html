Paper session

Start paper trading

Pick a category, then a live match or market from Kalshi’s public books. Choose a trading mode (Safe → Aggressive), set a paper bankroll, then Start. Paper signal: Avellaneda–Stoikov reservation price plus order-book imbalance, after Kalshi fees and spread. This is simulated cash — the UI cannot place live Kalshi or exchange orders. Not financial advice and not a match or price predictor. There is no guaranteed profitable model. can_size_up stays locked. Stop ends polling. Clear wipes the local paper session (fills, P&L, log, selected contract) — not a live Kalshi account.

Balanced mid-high — 2 contracts, 3¢ edge, $10/trade, 8¢ max spread, faster polls.

Hybrid/LLM read OPENAI_API_KEY from a local .env only — never paste keys here. ChatGPT is asked to anticipate score/momentum swings, not just restate odds. Research is not a guaranteed edge.
