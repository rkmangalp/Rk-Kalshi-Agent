Paper session

Start paper trading

Set a paper bankroll, then Start. Paper-trades Kalshi Bitcoin buy and sell (YES contracts) and optional live tennis. Optionally lock to any pasted Kalshi market or event. This is simulated cash — the UI cannot place live Kalshi or exchange orders. can_size_up stays locked. Stop ends polling. Clear wipes the local paper session (fills, P&L, log, selected contract) — not a live Kalshi account.

Contract / match

Choose a contract to paper-trade

If a contract is set, paper trading uses only that event’s markets — not every open book. Paste any Kalshi market or event URL, or pick a listed event.

No contract selected — paper can scan the full enabled universe.

Examples: https://kalshi.com/markets/kxatpmatch/atp-tennis-match/kxatpmatch-26sep06cerblo · https://kalshi.com/markets/kxatpchallengermatch/challenger-atp-/kxatpchallengermatch-26sep06kimtam · https://kalshi.com/markets/kxbtc15m/bitcoin-price-up-down/kxbtc15m-26sep061845
